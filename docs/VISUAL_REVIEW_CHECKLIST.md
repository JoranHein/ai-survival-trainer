# Visual Review Checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing screenshots or screen recordings of AI Survival Trainer.

## Readability

- Can I instantly find Ari?
- Can I read Ari's thought bubble?
- Can I understand what Ari is doing?
- Are enemies readable?
- Are structures readable?
- Is the day/night mood clear?
- Is the UI too crowded?

## Game Fantasy

- Is the sign-god fantasy visible?
- Does Ari feel like the focus, not just a player marker?
- Does Ari's emotion come through?
- Does the scene communicate survival pressure?
- Does the scene feel like a survival game, not a code demo?

## Ari

- Is Ari visually distinct from enemies and structures?
- Does Ari's current action read clearly?
- Does Ari's emotional state read through text, posture, color, animation, or context?
- Is the thought bubble placed where the player can connect it to Ari?
- Is the thought bubble meaningful rather than noisy?

## Enemies

- Can I tell enemy types apart?
- Can I tell whether enemies are threatening Ari, structures, or both?
- Is enemy motion direction understandable?
- Are attacks, damage, and danger moments readable?

## Structures

- Can I distinguish walls, towers, traps, wards, farms, and stations?
- Can I tell which structures are damaged?
- Can I tell which structures are active?
- Can I understand why a structure helped or failed?

## Day And Night

- Does dusk visibly increase tension?
- Does night feel dangerous without hiding essential information?
- Is gradual darkening visible?
- Does the player still understand Ari and enemies at night?

## UI

- Does the HUD show only the information needed for the current milestone?
- Is important survival info easier to find than debug info?
- Are debug panels useful without taking over the game view?
- Are labels readable at the target resolution?

## Final Review Question

What is the single highest-impact visual fix for this screenshot?
